HOW TO PLAY

Your mission

  1. Choose a Bible mystery.
  2. Read the clue and look at the illustration.
  3. Choose the correct character from four answers.
  4. Reveal another clue if you need help.
  5. Correct answers earn points. Wrong answers cost one life.

Scoring system

Clue 1: 10 points · Clue 2: 5 points · Clue 3: 2 points

You start with three lives. Try to solve all ten cases!
